Play Asia Map Quiz

How to play

Match the country name and flag to its location on the map. Click or tap to answer. Correct countries turn green. Wrong answers briefly identify the place you selected, and after three mistakes the answer pulses gold to guide you.

Scroll or pinch to zoom, drag to explore, or use the map controls. Small countries have enlarged targets. You can also use Tab and Enter or Space to choose countries.

Practice the countries you miss

Practice mode focuses on your mistakes, with no timer or score. Revisit all recorded mistakes, or practice the missed countries from a specific past attempt. The quiz score is countries found divided by total guesses.

Do I need an account?

No. Play for free in your browser. Your round, past attempts, mistakes, sound setting, and map theme are saved on this device. There is no shared leaderboard or cloud progress account.

Can I delete my history?

Yes. Open Past attempts to delete an attempt or clear the list. Clearing browser storage also removes saved game data. Progress does not sync across devices.

Make the map your own

Choose Ocean, Forest, Dusk, or Paper from the palette control. Mute the short sound effects whenever you like. Country names are never read aloud.
